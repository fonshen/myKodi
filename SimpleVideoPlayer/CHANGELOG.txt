Changelog - Cleanup and simplifications

- Replaced blocking MessageBox and Console.WriteLine calls with Debug.WriteLine or removed them.
- Removed verbose Debug.WriteLine logs from many ViewModels and Views to reduce noise.
- Made thumbnail and duration loading asynchronous to improve startup performance.
- Unified and simplified card/item styles in Themes\LargeButtonTheme.xaml; removed unused LargeListBoxItemStyle.
- Fixed focus/selection issues and card focus visual inconsistencies.
- Adjusted card sizing logic to correctly compute width taking margins and borders into account.
- Added ffmpeg-based fallback duration parsing when LibVLC fails, and background duration extraction.
- Removed redundant or unused debug outputs in RemoteControlService and other services.
